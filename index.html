How it works

Three rules. That's it.

1

Type your word

Any single word, one to twenty-four characters. Letters and numbers only.

2

One per email

Each email can write or grow one word, so the wall stays honest — no piling on.

3

Watch it grow

Every time someone else writes the same word, it gets bigger and bolder on the wall.

FAQ

Questions & answers

Can I add more than one word?

No — one word (or one "grow" click) per email, to keep the wall honest.

Why do you need my email?

Just to stop the same person growing a word over and over. It's hashed in your browser before it's ever sent, and the server never lets it be read back — only the words and counts are public.

What stops bots from spamming it?

A hidden field bots tend to auto-fill, plus a short delay before any submission counts. It filters out the lazy ones, though a determined bot could still get through — nothing client-side is bulletproof.

Does the wall ever reset?

No — it's stored in a real database and stays there for everyone who visits this page.
